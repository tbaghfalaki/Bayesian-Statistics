# Bayesian-Statistics

This page contains the R code accompanying the **Bayesian Statistics** Bookwork by Baghfalaki, from the *Department of Mathematics* at **the University of Manchester**, Semester 1 (2025–26).



## Introduction to the Course Unit
This Bayesian Statistics unit introduces the fundamental concepts and computational techniques of Bayesian inference. It is designed to develop your understanding of Bayesian methods for data analysis, model evaluation, and decision-making. Through a combination of lectures, tutorials, and computer-based practicals, you will gain both theoretical knowledge and hands-on experience applying Bayesian approaches to real-world statistical problems.

## Structure of Course Unit Materials and Activities
The unit is divided into two main parts:

Foundational Bayesian Inference – covering the core principles and methodology.
Computational Bayesian Statistics – focusing on practical implementation and advanced techniques. Up to Week 6, computations will primarily be performed using JAGS. From Week 8 onwards, students will transition to RStan and R-INLA, which are essential for advanced modeling and inference tasks. Working with all three platforms will also enhance students’ overall proficiency in Bayesian computation.

Teaching consists of 11 lectures (22 hours in total), 5 laboratory sessions (10 hours in total), and 6 tutorials (6 hours in total), all delivered in plenary format. This gives a total of 38 contact hours across the semester. Course materials, including reading lists, and computational exercises, will be provided online for flexible access throughout the semester.

## Recommended reading

The lecture notes for Bayesian statistics are comprehensive with many worked examples and no further reading is required to perform well on this part of the unit.

If, however, you wish to do some further reading, then some suitable books include:

Gelman, A., et al. (2013). Bayesian Data Analysis (3rd edition). – Covers both the theory and practice of Bayesian inference, with many real-world examples.

McElreath, R. (2020). Statistical Rethinking (2nd edition). – Introduces Bayesian modeling in an intuitive, example-driven way, ideal for understanding the conceptual ideas.

Kruschke, J. K. (2015). Doing Bayesian Data Analysis (2nd edition). – Hands-on approach with step-by-step Bayesian workflows, emphasizing conceptual understanding.

Robert, C. P. (2007). The Bayesian Choice (2nd edition). – A more formal treatment, explaining the foundational ideas and decision-theoretic basis of Bayesian inference.

Christensen, R., Johnson, W., Branscum, A., & Hanson, T. E. (2010). Bayesian Ideas and Data Analysis: An Introduction for Scientists and Statisticians. CRC Press. – Accessible introduction for scientists and statisticians, focusing on Bayesian concepts and applied examples.
